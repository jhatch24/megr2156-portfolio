# A7 – FEA on Bracket

## **Objective**  
The main objective of this assignment is to use Finite Element Analysis to improve the bracket design from previous two assignments. Doing this will ensure high stiffness, and lightweight optimization through iterative design, whilst simultaneously conducting multiple FEA simulations, refining geometry based on results. In addition, perform static stress analysis with constraints, loading, and meshing sections. Identifying maximum stress, displacement and safety factors to optimize design by modifying geometry to enhance performance. Lastly, documenting the entire process with images and calculations to help reflect on key engineering lessons learned and total time spent on the project.  

## **Analyze**  
Firstly, I started by running a static simulation in Solidworks for my first iteration of my design and seeing where my base line was. This was purely to see where I could improve, simulate my part to failure, and see where I optimize the overall geometry of my part.  
Computations for each design to determine converengence of less than 7 % difference between each FEA mesh changes.

## **Decide**  
I decided that I was going to use Design 3 as my final design for my motor mount because it demonstrated light weight capabilities, underwent the maximum deflection and stress compared to the first two design loops, as well as is the part with the highest safety factor amongst the three. I believe my 2nd design truly demonstrated being pushed till failure because it was the weakest in all statistics by far. Although some weight was optimized, all other parts suffered because of it.  

## **Communicate**  
In total over the course of the week I spent around 8 hours doing calculations, designing, running simulation loops multiple times for each design iteration, and lastly writing and updating page accurately represent my assignment and research. I believe the key engineering lessons learnt during this process was to not hold back on simulating a part to failure to truly test its boundaries, as well as learning to think about why I am actually drawing lines and getting rid of material. Each decision has an impact and must be throughout thoroughly because safety can be at risk.
